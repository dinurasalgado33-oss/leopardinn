Leopard Inn began with a simple idea — a room shouldn't separate you from the landscape it sits in. Every villa is raised on a deck, wrapped in glass, and finished in warm timber, with a private plunge pool close enough to reach from the bed.

The idea

Two branches, two very different landscapes. At Arugam Bay, the deck looks straight out to the ocean. At Wilpattu, it looks out over the scrub jungle that gives Sri Lanka's leopards their name. Same handmade teak furniture, same quiet attention to detail — different horizon.

● Arugam Bay

Deck-out, pool-in, ocean right there.

An elevated timber villa a few steps from the sand, with a private plunge pool on the deck and the Indian Ocean as the view from bed. Built for surf mornings and slow, salty evenings.
