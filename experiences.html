Wilpattu

Leopard country, and the river

Wilpattu jeep safari

Wilpattu is the largest national park in Sri Lanka and one of the best places on earth to see a leopard. The villa sits near the gate, which is the whole point — the park opens at first light, and being among the first vehicles through it changes what the day looks like.

Sightings take patience here. Wilpattu is bigger and far quieter than the southern parks, so a leopard is something you find rather than something you queue for.

  • Jeep and driver-tracker
  • Park tickets and permits
  • Dawn and afternoon drives
  • Full-day option
  • Leopards, sloth bears, elephants
  • Back for the pool

Arugam Bay

Kumana, the point breaks, and the sand

Kumana safari, from Arugam Bay

Kumana sits south of Arugam Bay and is one of the best birding parks in the country — its mangrove lagoon draws thousands of migratory birds through the season. There are leopards here too, and elephants, and a fraction of the vehicles you would share a sighting with further south.

Same arrangement as Wilpattu. We book the jeep and the tracker, handle the tickets, and get you to the gate for opening.

  • Jeep and driver-tracker
  • Park tickets and permits
  • Dawn and afternoon drives
  • Leopards and elephants
  • Exceptional birdlife
