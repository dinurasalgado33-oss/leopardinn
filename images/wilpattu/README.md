# Wilpattu — photo drop

Put Wilpattu photos in this folder. Anything here is fair game for the website and for social posts.

**Just drop files in — no prep needed.**

- Any format: JPG, PNG, or iPhone HEIC (HEIC gets converted automatically).
- Straight off the camera or phone is ideal — the bigger the original, the better. Cropping and compressing happens on the way out, so nothing here needs resizing first.
- Filenames don't matter. `IMG_4829.jpg` is fine.

**What makes a photo usable:**

- Portrait or square framing survives Instagram's 4:5 crop best. Landscape still works, it just gets cropped tighter.
- The A-frame silhouette and the river deck are the strongest visual assets here — shots that show the villa *in* the jungle beat interiors alone.
- Warm natural light. Golden hour and soft daylight beat midday glare or flash.

Worth a quick glance before dropping: tools, cables, laundry, or construction materials in frame usually mean the shot has to be cropped hard or skipped. If it's the only shot of something, include it anyway and it can be cropped around.
